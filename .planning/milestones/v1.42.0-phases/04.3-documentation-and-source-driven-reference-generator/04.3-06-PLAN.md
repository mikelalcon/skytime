## expression_if.star (Phase 04.2 expression-mode demo, CANONICAL TUTORIAL)

- **What:** Three flows demonstrating procedural-mode if_cond, expression-mode if_cond with both-result branches, and asymmetric (one-result/one-fail) branches
- **Features:** flow, step, script, if_cond (BOTH procedural and expression mode), result(value=...), top-level fail("..."), ${ctx.expr} interpolation in fail messages, output_alias

  1. `procedural_demo` — procedural-mode preserved (Phase 4 + 04.1 syntax)
  2. `classify_repo_size` — both branches end in result(), bound to ctx.classification
