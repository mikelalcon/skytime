## Two-Tier Model Visibility

Both landings make the two-tier authoring model visible. The flow-authors landing closes:

> Many flow-author teams pair with a Go-side teammate who builds extensions; the consultant team writes the `.star` files. This is the **two-tier authoring model** in action: extensions are reusable across customers, flows are specialized per customer, and the parse/execute boundary keeps the two tiers honest.

The extension-developers landing closes:

> The two tiers are separated by the parse/execute boundary — your Go code runs at parse time (registering operations and binding the `Initialize`-returned module as a Starlark global) and inside the single generic activity (executing operations against `context.Context` with the credential resolved JIT). The Starlark side never sees Go beyond the typed kwargs/output shapes you declare.
