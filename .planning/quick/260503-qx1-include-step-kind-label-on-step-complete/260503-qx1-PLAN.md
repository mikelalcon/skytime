<objective>
Mirror the step_dispatch shape onto the step_complete line so user-defined step
names (Phase 04.1 D4.1-15 `step(name="Get repo ${ctx.repo}")`) persist after
completion. The label currently appears only on the dispatch banner and during
the live spinner redraw; after the step finishes, the finalized row drops the
label and shows just `[1/3] step  ✓ 234ms  status=200`. Desired:
`[1/3] step                Get repo octocat/Hello-World  ✓ 234ms  status=200`.

ROUTE CHOSEN: **(a) interpreter emits `label` on step_complete events**. All
seven `logger.Info("skytime", "event", "step_complete", ...)` call sites in
pkg/interpreter (walk_step ×2, walk_ifcond, walk_script, walk_foreach ×2,
walk_callflow) gain a `"label", <label>` attribute pair, exactly mirroring
their existing step_dispatch shape. The renderer side is then a stateless
read of `attrs.str("label")` / `ev.Label` (the latter is already populated by
buildProgressEvent — pkg/cli/progress.go line 280, no change needed there).

Why route (a) over (b):
  - Stateless. No `labelByIdx` map on *progressHandler / *liveRenderer to
    manage across WithAttrs/WithGroup shallow-copies.
  - Symmetric. Every dispatch event already emits `label`; the absence on
    step_complete is the bug, not a design choice.
  - One-line-per-call-site change. All 7 emit sites have `label` already in
    scope (a local variable or a struct field they're already reading for
    dispatch, or trivially derivable for the for_each error path).
  - Renderer changes remain in scope: switching the static + live renderers
    to consume `label` is the load-bearing display work; the interpreter
    change just feeds the data they need.

Purpose: User said `step(name="...")` and meant it — the name is the
identity of the step, and identity must persist through the entire row's
lifecycle (dispatch → spinning → completion). Anything less defeats the
D4.1-15 feature and the demo flow's `${ctx.repo}` interpolation.

Output: A skytime binary whose `skytime run examples/skeleton/simple_check.star
--input '{"repo":"octocat/Hello-World"}'` execution shows the step name on
both the live spinner row AND the finalized ✓ row, with column alignment.
</objective>

    idx := a.int("idx")

    kind := a.str("kind")

        p.colorCounter(counter),
        p.colorKind(padKind(kind)),
        label,
